The candle opens the existing desk inspector with the exact Smithsonian exhibition print. Its 800 × 697 image is presented as a small overview. Fine-detail magnification is limited by the source file. The reader credits Roake & Varty and the museum, links to the exhibition and terms, and distinguishes head spermaceti from blubber oil.

The desk artwork and its three existing document readers are preserved. The candle is a fourth physical discovery. Escape or Close returns focus to the candle at the same scroll depth. Keyboard, reduced-motion and phone-size checks cover all four control bounds and separation.

The founder deferred the storyboard and asked to use the smaller museum reproduction. No generated artwork, storyboard or film is included. [Source and future asset manifest](../../price-of-light-assets.md).

Review used Chromium at 1440 × 900, 390 × 844 and 360 × 844. Physical iPhones and Safari remain unverified.
