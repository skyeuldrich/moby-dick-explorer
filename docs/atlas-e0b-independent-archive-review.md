# Atlas E0b — independent archival-source review and design recommendation

**18 November 2026 (America/New_York); status: research delivered for founder review, not approved artwork and not implementation authorization.** This supplements [E0 storyboard](atlas-e0-editorial-design-handoff.md), [initial source shortlist](atlas-e0b-archival-chart-shortlist.md), and [Copilot's merged E0b report](atlas-e0b-verification-and-mockups.md) in [Issue #27](https://github.com/skyeuldrich/moby-dick-explorer/issues/27).

## Scope and evidence quality

This pass inspected **public catalogue records, item-level Wikimedia Commons file descriptions, original scan dimensions/rights labels, and searchable image previews**. It did **not** acquire source JPEG bytes or conduct a full-resolution pixel/legibility/crop audit: outbound archive hosts could not be resolved from the local runtime. The previous Copilot agent reported the same image-access failure. The existing two map-backed Heather mockups in PR #30 remain the only verified-image mockups; its other ocean mockups are placeholders. Do not represent this report, a web thumbnail, or the placeholder boards as actual approved source-backed art.

**Strong distinction:** a catalogue title can establish a *published intended region*, not every chart's legible boundaries. A Commons *Public Domain Mark* is a documented file-page rights classification, **not automatically an independently validated legal conclusion** for every country. For BnF scans, verify Gallica's source terms if taking files from Gallica rather than Commons. For downloaded Rumsey files, its linked **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0** terms and publication permissions require explicit provenance/compatibility review. Record exact file, rights, credit, and SHA-256 before use. No new archival image or website code was committed in this research pass.

## Recommendations and differences from previous E0b plan

1. **Do not declare BnF Blunt 1840 a complete west-and-east South Atlantic route background.** Portrait/region mismatch requires pixel verification; full Blunt 1834 remains a valuable *historical object* if the whaler annotations' rights and legibility are cleared. The 1739 Dépôt de la Marine chart is a credible full-basin, public-domain-classified alternative.
2. **A single “Indian Ocean” folio can contain two or more physical source sheets**: wide ocean basin, then the 1813 Horsburgh Sunda close-up, then a China Sea transition as geographic scale narrows. This is intentional chaptering, not a forged stitched projection.
3. **Build the educational pivot around Ch. 87's grammar**: “purposing to pass” denotes intention rather than a logged full passage. Give the visitor the narrated-only and conjectural overlays to inspect. Later Bashee references are evidence of subsequent position, not a complete daily ship record.
4. **For the western Pacific, source authenticity outranks an attractive modern composite.** Norie's *northern* sheet can show Japan, but do not reuse the installed far-eastern southern Pacific crop. At the end, draw only a broad equatorial *evidence zone*; no point for the sinking.
5. **Avoid double attribution errors**: publisher's printed routes and captain's actual pencil tracks belong to their original artifact; the website's red hypothetical course and Chapter 44 graphite are distinct modern overlays.
6. **No automatic merge or “finished” label:** PR #30 was merged with review placeholders and without founder image approval. Keep this new pass research-only and continue E0b gating before creating coding assignments.
