## Why this matters
What changes when different people read the same object? The chapter moves
through speeches and observations rather than offering a single narrator's
explanation. A first reader may see every reading as equally free invention;
the coin's described peaks, sun and zodiac also constrain what readers notice.

## Two readings grounded in the text
1. **The coin as mirror:** Ahab calls each peak himself, while Starbuck sees
   possible trinitarian imagery and hope shadowed by its imagined absence at
   midnight. Their
   different concerns shape what the same figures mean. But Starbuck's reading
   is not simply optimistic: he fears truth may “shake me falsely.”
2. **The coin as material prize:** Flask values the gold at sixteen dollars and
   calculates what it can buy, refusing the others' symbolic elaborations.
   This grounds interpretation in the hunt's economic incentive. Yet even
   his calculation is a perspective, not an escape from interpretation.

## Educational experience
- **30 seconds:** show brief speaker-labeled excerpts of Ahab and Starbuck's
  responses alongside the three peaks. Ask what each noticed and link to their
  sequential passages and the complete chapter. No verdict chooses the
  “correct” reader.
- **Alternative:** contrast Flask's price calculation with Ahab's symbolic
  reading, keeping the hunt's material reward visible.
- **3 minutes:** add Stubb, his almanac, and his mediated accounts of others;
  ask how much can be inferred when someone else speaks for them.
- Objective: connect interpretive differences to specific shared details.
  Offer text-first keyboard controls, readable labels, and no-motion access;
  do not imply that all possible interpretations are equally supported.
- Check: can a visitor name one shared feature and two different uses of it?
  Trial with new and current readers and teachers: record cited evidence,
  chapter-link use and reasons for stopping, plus mobile, keyboard and screen
  reader access. Link use alone is not proof of learning. This is a proposed
