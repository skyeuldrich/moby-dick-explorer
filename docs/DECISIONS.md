## How to update this log

When a decision changes, **add a replacement entry or revise the relevant row with the new date, rationale, and link**. Do not quietly erase the fact that an earlier choice governed shipped work. Keep speculative possibilities in issues or creative briefs until approved; they are **not** decisions.

**Historical material:** older `moby-dick-prototype-brief-v0*.md` and `docs/next-visual-push-execution-plan.md` explain prior work, but do not override [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) or a new founder-approved issue.
